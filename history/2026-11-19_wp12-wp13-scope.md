## Deploy-day trap worth carrying forward

Three separate things on this host are "the value is the file, not the variable": the git-crypt key,
the `dbops_password_*` seeds, and the `lookup('password', 'vars/.<name>')` seeds. A **new git worktree
of the canonical checkout is safe for git-crypt** (the keys live under `.git/worktrees/<name>/git-crypt`
once copied) but **not** for the generated seeds: WP-12's first deploy rendered a freshly generated
`DB_PASS` and crash-looped Moodle for ~6 minutes (full write-up in the WP-12 history entry). Copy the
`ansible/plays/vars/.<name>` files from the canonical checkout into the worktree *before* the first
deploy, and compare their sha256 if unsure.

## Do not undo

- Do not re-run a deploy from a checkout whose `plays/vars/.<name>` seeds differ from the canonical
  checkout.
- Do not treat this branch as having touched R10: the `ls_stage_restore` role, the restic units and the
  script directories are exactly as they were.
